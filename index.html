Why manual?

Tracking isn't supposed to disappear.

When you enter a purchase yourself, you actually see it. That small moment is the point: it's how you start to notice where your money goes.

L3dgr makes that moment fast enough to be effortless, without making it invisible.

Automatic tracking

  • Hand over your bank login to a third party

  • Easy to set up, easy to stop looking at

Questions

Good to know.

Can I connect my bank account?

No, and that's on purpose. L3dgr is built around entering what you spend yourself. There are no bank logins, no syncing and no waiting for transactions.

Isn't typing every purchase a chore?

Adding one takes a few seconds: amount, category, done. Bills and other regular payments can be set up once as recurring items, so you only type the one-off spending.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Your entries, goals and settings stay in the app's private storage and aren't sent to us. You can export a backup file at any time and keep it wherever you like. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. There's no sign-up, and tracking works offline. The internet is only used for purchases through Google Play and for sending feedback.

What happens if I change phones?

Export a backup from Your data, move the file to your new phone and import it. Everything comes across, including your card layout.

Is the free version really free?

Yes. Tracking, goals, recurring items, analytics and backups are all free, with no time limit. Plus and Pro add colour themes, backgrounds and extras.

Is there an iPhone, web or desktop version?

L3dgr is on Android first. A web and desktop app is coming, as part of Pro. If you'd like to hear when it's ready, write to hello@l3dgr.app.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In Google Play: Profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, and your data is never affected.
